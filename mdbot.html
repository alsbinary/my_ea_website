Step-by-Step Workflow

How Does MDbot Work?

1
Create & Connect Deriv Account:

First, create your Deriv account via Official Deriv Registration Link. If you already have an account, simply login to connect securely via OAuth 2.0 PKCE gateway.

2
Configure Strategy Levels:

Setup sequential trading levels (Level 1, Level 2, etc.) specifying individual contracts, markets, durations, barrier offsets, and stake amounts.

3
Execution Logic (Win / Loss Rules):

Trading starts at Level 1. On a WIN, the bot automatically resets back to Level 1. On a LOSS, it moves to Level 2 and beyond according to your strategy rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with MDbot?

Create your account using the Official Deriv Registration Link. If you already have an account, log in directly via the bot dashboard.

Do I need coding experience to build a strategy?

No. MDbot provides an intuitive visual interface where you configure markets, stake amounts, durations, and trade types for each level directly using forms.

How is Net Profit calculated in the bot?

Net Profit is calculated automatically by subtracting your stake amount from the total payout returned by the Deriv proposal API.
